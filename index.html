Ear Mirror

See inside your ear, with spoken directions to line up the phone when you can’t see the screen.

🔒Everything runs on this device. The page never uploads, stores, or sends your camera images. Photos disappear when you close the page.

Which ear?

No sound? Raise the volume, turn off silent mode on iPhone, then tap Test voice.

Photo option: if the camera is blocked, take a picture with your phone’s camera app and pick it here. You’ll still get the check, without live voice guidance.

Tip: set the phone volume up, and stand by a window or in a bright room. Hold the phone about a hand’s width from your ear, back camera facing it.

Result

Press and hold the photo to save it to your phone.

This is a rough color-based estimate, not a medical test. A phone can’t see deep into the canal. Don’t push cotton swabs inside. If you have pain, discharge, a blocked feeling, or hearing changes, see a doctor or pharmacist.
